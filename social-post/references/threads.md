# Threads

> last_verified: 2026-08-24
> scope: durable writing and measurement workflow.

- Lead with one observation, claim or contrast.
- Prefer native, readable prose; do not turn a short post into a dashboard of hooks and CTAs.
- A thread should advance one argument per post and make the first post useful on its own.
- Treat repost, quote, reply, profile visit and follow as different outcomes.
- Topic, author baseline and network seeding are confounds; one viral post does not prove a portable formula.
- Record published copy and time, then compare snapshots at similar maturity.
