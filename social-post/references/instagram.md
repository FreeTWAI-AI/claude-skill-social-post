## Reels and posts

- The opening visual must communicate the object, conflict or payoff without relying on the caption.
- Caption can help classification, search and conversion, but cannot repair weak watch behavior.
- Use only relevant hashtags; their count is an experiment, not a timeless rule.

## Publishing

- Confirm the selected media, crop, cover, audio, caption and account before sending.
- If cross-posting is enabled, save platform scopes independently when insights arrive.

## Measurement

Separate views／plays, reached accounts, average watch, skip, traffic sources, profile actions and follows. Preserve whether the UI value is rounded.
