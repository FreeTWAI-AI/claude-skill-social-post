## Packaging

- Make the subject and payoff legible in the first frame.

## Measurement

Keep views, engaged views, unique viewers, average view duration, percentage viewed, subscribers and revenue distinct.
YouTube may abbreviate interface values; use `metric_qualifiers: rounded` instead of converting the display to false precision.
Search, Shorts feed, browse and external sources are distribution evidence, not proof that one keyword caused the result.
